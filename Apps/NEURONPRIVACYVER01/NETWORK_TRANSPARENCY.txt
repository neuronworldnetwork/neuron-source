Neuron Network Transparency Statement (verifier companion)
==========================================================

Neuron World Network states that the Privacy Verifier’s declared infrastructure
list (infra_allowlist.json) enumerates non-peer destinations the product may
contact for IP discovery, call setup assist, connectivity checks, optional
weather/search/news helpers, and user-configured mail/share providers.

Neuron does not operate a hidden telemetry / analytics phone-home endpoint as
part of normal mesh operation. Any unexplained outbound destination should
appear as REVIEW in the verifier and may be reported to neuron-helpdesk@proton.me.

This statement is versioned with the verifier allowlist. Auditors should compare
observed categories against infra_allowlist.json in this folder.
